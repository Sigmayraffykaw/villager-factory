Inside the castle

Four departments. One giant build.

Keep the villagers split into manageable groups. A large population can cause heavy lag, and villagers need valid paths, food, beds and accessible job-site blocks for the intended behaviour.

⛓️

Worker cells

Individual trading/work stalls with a reachable job-site block. Beds are optional for ordinary trading, but provide them where your layout requires sleeping.

🌱

Breeding wing

Adult villagers, reliable food supply and spare valid beds. Baby villagers need a route that doesn't block the adults from their beds.

🌾

Crop greenhouses

Wheat, carrots or potatoes with water and lighting. A farmer needs to be able to reach crops and collect food.

🛏️

Bed chambers

Provide enough valid beds for the intended population. Keep the bed space clear and make sure villagers can pathfind to the beds.

🫧

Baby collection

Plan a separate corridor or collection room. Test every water stream and drop before allowing villagers into the system.

🧱

Ocean foundation

A flat, solid platform helps with placement. Leave room for the 81-block castle footprint and access paths around the edges.

Build sequence

Build it in this order.

The current repository contains a site concept and viewer. A validated downloadable .litematic schematic has not yet been added, so don't treat the 3D preview as a ready-to-paste schematic.

Foundation

Make a flat 100 × 100 platform, then mark an 81 × 81 footprint in the centre.

Castle shell

Build the walls, corner towers, entrances and roof. Keep maintenance corridors accessible.

Test one wing

Test a small breeder, one worker cell and the baby route in Creative first.

Scale slowly

Add groups gradually and monitor performance before aiming for 1,000 villagers.
